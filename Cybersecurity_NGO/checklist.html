Before you click…

🎯 One improvement for my NGO next week

Think of the Escape Room and the Bingo: which weak point does your NGO have? Write one thing you will change next week – it stays on this phone only.

Tip: bookmark this page or take a screenshot.

🛑 Stop · Check · Report

  • Sender – is the address exactly right? (gmaii.com ≠ gmail.com)
  • Link – read the address before the first “/”: who really owns it?
  • Request – money, a password, a code, a changed bank account?
  • Pressure – urgency, secrecy, “don't call me”?
  • Too good / too scary – a donation, a blocked account, a parcel?
In doubt? Don't click. Call the person on a number you already know, and tell your IT contact.

✅ 3 habits

MFA on your email + a password manager
Share with named people only
One backup copy kept offline (3-2-1)

🚨 If something goes wrong

  1. Disconnect the device
  2. Change your password from another device
  3. Tell your IT contact or board, then report it (see Get help)
