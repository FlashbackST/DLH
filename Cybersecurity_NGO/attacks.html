4 attacks, 3 tricks

1 · Phishing

A fake email that tries to make you click, pay or give a password.

From: PayFund Donations <noreply@payfund-donations.info>
Subject: You received a €2,500 donation!

A donor has sent your organisation €2,500. To accept the funds, open the attached receipt and log in within 48 hours.

📎 Donation_Receipt.html
  • Too good to be true
  • A deadline: “within 48 hours”
  • Log in through an attachment – a real receipt never asks for that

2 · Smishing

SMS + phishing: the same trick, by text message.

SecurePay
Your account has been blocked for security reasons. Confirm your identity today: securepay-verify.top
  • Fear: “account blocked”
  • Urgency: “today”
  • Strange link – not the official website

3 · Deepfakes

A computer can copy a voice or a face from a few seconds of video found online.

📞 Unknown number
“Hi, it's Marc, the president. I need €4,800 sent to a new partner right now. Keep it between us.”
  • Money + urgency + secrecy
  • Unknown number – hang up and call back on the number you know

4 · Social engineering & LinkedIn

Attackers manipulate people instead of computers. They read what you post publicly, then write a message that sounds real.

🔎 What the attacker finds online:
“Anna – new treasurer at Les Amis du Parc” · “Jean – president, at a conference this week”
To: Anna
Subject: Welcome Anna! Urgent payment before Friday

Hi Anna, congratulations on your new role! Jean asked me to send you this invoice – he's at the conference and can't be reached…

  • Uses real names and roles from public profiles
  • Uses the president's absence to stop you checking
💡 Think before you post: new roles, team lists and absences help attackers.

Remember the 3 tricks

⏰
Urgency
“Now”, “today”, “within 48h”
👔
Authority
The president, the bank, “IT support”
🎁
Curiosity
A donation, a parcel, a shared file
