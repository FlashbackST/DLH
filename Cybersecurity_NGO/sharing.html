Share safely & back up
Part 5 · Share safely & back up

Share only with who needs it

✉️ Email

Double-check the recipient before you send. Don't send personal data to private addresses. Use “Bcc” for group emails to donors or volunteers.

☁️ Cloud storage

Share with named people, never “anyone with the link”. Remove access when the work is done.

🗂️ Shared drives

Give each person only what they need. Review who has access when volunteers or staff leave.

💬 Messaging apps

No beneficiary or donor data in WhatsApp or other group chats. Use the NGO's own tools for personal data.

Keep a copy they can't reach

Ransomware locks all your files and asks for money to unlock them. A good backup lets you recover without paying.

💾 The 3-2-1 backup rule

3copies of your data
2different places (e.g. cloud + external disk)
1copy kept offline

Synced folders and disks that stay plugged in get locked too. Only an offline copy saves you – and test that you can restore it.

🚨 Ransomware: the first 3 things to do

  1. Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi and the network.
  2. Don't pay and don't delete anything.
  3. Call your IT contact or board right away, then report it (see Get help).
