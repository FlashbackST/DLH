Why NGOs are targeted

Small does not mean safe

💎 NGOs hold valuable data

Names, addresses and bank details of donors · contact details of members · often sensitive information about beneficiaries (health, family, social situation).

🧰 …with little protection

Often no IT department · volunteers using their own phones and laptops · shared passwords · little time for security.

💥 What an attack can cost

  • Money – a fake invoice paid, donations diverted
  • Data – beneficiaries' and donors' information stolen or published
  • Trust – donors and partners lose confidence in your NGO
  • Time – days or weeks to get systems working again
