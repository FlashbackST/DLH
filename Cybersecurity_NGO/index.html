Cybersecurity for NGOs
Cybersecurity for NGOs · Session 1

Recognising and avoiding common threats

NGOs hold personal data about donors, members and beneficiaries, but often have little IT support. Today you learn how the most common attacks work – and the everyday habits that stop them.

At the end of the session, you can…

1
Recognise the most common attacks on NGOs: phishing, smishing and social engineering.
2
Apply basic security habits: MFA, password managers, checking links, safe sharing.
3
Identify the three main weak points in your NGO's daily work.
