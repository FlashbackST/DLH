Part 4 · Passwords & MFA

Lock your accounts

🔑 Strong passwords

  1. One long, unique password per account. A short sentence is easy to remember: “Our-garden-opens-in-May!”
  2. Use a password manager. It remembers all your passwords – you only remember one.
  3. Never reuse your email password anywhere else.

📱 Turn on MFA – follow the trainer

MFA (multi-factor authentication) = your password + a code or an approval on your phone. Like a bank card: knowing the PIN is not enough, you also need the card.

  1. Open your email account settings – start with email, it unlocks everything else.
  2. Go to Security → 2-step verification or Two-factor authentication.
  3. Choose an authenticator app (or SMS) and follow the steps.
  4. Save the backup codes somewhere safe.
⚠️ An MFA request you didn't start? Deny it, change your password and tell your IT contact.

🤝 Help a less technical colleague

  1. Explain why in one sentence: “It protects the donor data we are responsible for.”
  2. Sit together for 10 minutes and do it on their phone – start with email.
  3. Check in a week later: does it work? Any questions?
