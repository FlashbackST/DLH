Backups & ransomware

Keep a copy they can't reach

Ransomware locks all your files and asks for money to unlock them. A good backup lets you recover without paying.

💾 The 3-2-1 backup rule

3copies of your data
2different places (e.g. cloud + external disk)
1copy kept offline

Synced folders and disks that stay plugged in get locked too. Only an offline copy saves you – and test that you can restore it.

🚨 Ransomware: the first 3 things to do

  1. Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi and the network.
  2. Don't pay and don't delete anything.
  3. Call your IT contact or board right away, then report it (see Get help).
