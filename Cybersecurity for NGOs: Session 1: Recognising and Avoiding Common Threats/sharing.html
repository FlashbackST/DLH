Share safely

Share only with who needs it

✉️ Email

Double-check the recipient before you send. Don't send personal data to private addresses. Use “Bcc” for group emails to donors or volunteers.

☁️ Cloud storage

Share with named people, never “anyone with the link”. Remove access when the work is done.

🗂️ Shared drives

Give each person only what they need. Review who has access when volunteers or staff leave.

💬 Messaging apps

No beneficiary or donor data in WhatsApp or other group chats. Use the NGO's own tools for personal data.
